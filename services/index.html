What we fix at your home or work

Pinoy Mobile Mechanic brings the repair to wherever your car is parked, anywhere within 50 miles of Mira Mesa. You get a written quote before any work starts.

  • Brake repairSquealing, grinding, a soft pedal or a car that pulls when you stop. Pads, rotors, calipers and brake fluid service, done where the car is parked.
  • Battery, starter and alternatorA car that clicks, cranks slowly or won't start, or a battery light on the dash. We find which part failed before anything gets replaced.
  • Check engine light and diagnosticsWarning lights, electrical problems, no-starts and strange noises. We find the actual cause and explain it in plain words.
  • Car A/C serviceAir blowing warm, weak airflow or odd smells from the vents. A/C diagnosis and repair in your driveway.
  • Hybrid service and repairHybrid warning lights and hybrid-specific maintenance, handled by a mechanic who works on hybrids.
  • Oil change and fluidsConventional, synthetic blend and full synthetic oil changes, plus the other fluids your car depends on, without a trip to a quick-lube line.
  • Belts, suspension and tune-upsSqueals at start-up, clunks over bumps, rough running and lost fuel economy, diagnosed and repaired on site.
