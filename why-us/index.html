Why San Diego drivers trust us

Pinoy Mobile Mechanic is a local, family-run mobile auto repair business based in Mira Mesa. We bring the shop to your driveway or work lot, tell you what's wrong in plain words, and put the price in writing before we touch anything.

  • Licensed in CaliforniaPinoy Mobile Mechanic LLC is registered with the California Bureau of Automotive Repair, license ARD 315968. You can look it up on the Bureau of Automotive Repair website.
  • A written quote before any workYou see the price in writing and approve it before a repair starts. If something else comes up, we ask again first. Nothing gets done without your OK.
  • Straight answersWe explain what's wrong without the jargon. If a job really needs a shop with a lift, we tell you so instead of guessing in your driveway.
  • We come to youHome, apartment lot or office parking lot, anywhere within 50 miles of Mira Mesa. That covers all of San Diego County, Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 5 PM.
  • A real person answersCall or text 858-933-7045 and you talk to the mechanic, not a call center.
  • Free, honest guidesOur car repair guides explain what common problems mean, so you know what you're dealing with before you book anyone.

How a visit works

  1. Book online or call. Tell us the address, the vehicle and what it's doing.
  2. We find the cause. The van arrives equipped, and we diagnose the problem where the car is parked.
  3. You approve a written quote. No surprises.
  4. We fix it on the spot. Most jobs are done right there while you keep working.
